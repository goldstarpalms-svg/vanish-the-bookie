# Other Sports Predictors Online — Deep Study for Vanish The Bookie

> Date: 2026-09-22 | No push, study only | Builds on SAFERSTAKE_STUDY.md

## 1. The Landscape — What Users Expect in 2026

From 10+ predictor sites analyzed, the modern user expects:
- **Free daily predictions** (today/tomorrow/weekend)
- **Probability %** (not just categorical pick)
- **Multiple markets** (1X2, Over/Under 2.5, BTTS, Double Chance, Correct Score, Corners, Cards, HT/FT)
- **Stats depth** (form last 5-10, H2H, xG, goals, home/away)
- **Transparency** (public archive, graded W/L, downloadable CSV, GitHub)
- **Tools** (accumulator builder, odds converter, team comparator, stake calc)
- **Coverage** (100+ to 1500+ leagues)
- **Live** (live predictions, live tracking)

**Accuracy reality check (from research):**
- Best models get **52-53%** match outcomes (2017 Soccer Prediction Challenge, 200k games trained)
- Bookmaker closing odds (Pinnacle) = **56-58%** most accurate predictor (market consensus)
- Claims of 70-90% are usually: Over/Under market (60-65% achievable), or curated Safe Tips low-odds, or self-reported unverified
- Honest sites publish **every pick graded green/red**, state settlement rules (90 min), shortlist vs all fixtures, admit weakness (e.g., "strongest on goal totals, weakest on winner")

- PredictZ 7/10 — AI-based
- BetEnsured 6/10, SportyTrader 6/10, SoccerVista 5/10, Vitibet 5/10

**Five checks for accuracy (from RighterOfWords):**
1. Which market does number cover? Need market, sample size, date range
2. Is there dated public archive? List of individual past predictions with dates, not just badge
3. Does each row show prediction as it stood before kickoff? Frozen pre-match snapshot
4. Does site say what counts and how it settles? Every fixture or shortlist? 90 min or extra time?
5. Does site say where model is weak? Which market strongest/weakest?

Few sites pass all 5. Superior Tips does: graded archive with frozen snapshot, states forecast fixed when published never recalculated, says what enters record is shortlisted selections not every fixture, settled on 90 min, says model strongest on goal totals and tight low-scoring games weakest at picking winner, daily selections drawn only from markets and price range where graded record holds up, probability fitted to record rather than raw model so it promises slightly less than lands.
